AI Draft

The key innovation was the self-attention mechanism. Rather than processing a sequence step by step, self-attention allows the model to look at all positions simultaneously and compute how much each position should "attend to" every other position.


Think of it this way: when you read a sentence, you don't forget the beginning by the time you reach the end. You hold the entire sentence in mind and understand how each word relates to every other word. Self-attention gives neural networks this same ability.


This approach has several concrete advantages over RNNs:

- Parallelization: all positions can be processed simultaneously on a GPU

- Long-range dependencies: any two positions are directly connected, regardless of distance

- Interpretability: attention weights reveal which parts of the input the model focuses on

Draft for "Multi-Head Attention":

The Transformer doesn't rely on a single attention function — it uses multiple "heads" running in parallel, each learning to focus on different types of relationships.

Why does this matter? A single attention head might learn to track syntactic relationships (which word is the subject of a verb), but miss semantic ones (which concepts are related). By running 8 or 16 heads simultaneously, the model captures multiple relationship types at once.

Research has shown that different heads naturally specialize: some track positional patterns, others capture coreference (linking "it" back to the noun it refers to), and others focus on semantic similarity.
