Attention Mechanisms
Auto-maintained by Synapse · Last updated Jun 28
Synthesized Knowledge

Self-attention allows models to weigh all positions in a sequence simultaneously, replacing the sequential processing of RNNs. Multi-head attention runs parallel attention operations, each learning different relationship types (syntactic, semantic, positional).

This mechanism is the core of the Transformer architecture and the foundation for BERT and modern NLP models.
